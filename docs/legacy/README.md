# Archived reports

The three reports in this folder (`BRIEF_SUMMARY.md`, `PROJECT_REPORT.md`,
`RAPPORT_ANALYSE_ML.md`) were the original project write-ups. They are kept
for history but **should not be trusted or cited**: their numbers (e.g.
"87.2% ROC-AUC", a 4-decimal confusion matrix, "45 columns", "2.3M apps
after cleaning", 6 models compared with 6 CV metrics each) do not match what
the accompanying notebook actually computed. The notebook only ever
cross-validated on `scoring='accuracy'` despite defining a 6-metric dict,
trained 3 models (not 6 -- `SVC`, `GradientBoostingClassifier` and
`GaussianNB` were imported but never fit), and never called `SMOTE` even
though `imbalanced-learn` was installed for that purpose. There is no
run of that notebook that produces the figures quoted in these reports.

The current, reproducible results live in `../../README.md` and
`../../artifacts/metrics.json`, generated by `python -m playstore.train`.
Treat anything in this folder as historical narrative only, not as a source
of truth about model performance.
